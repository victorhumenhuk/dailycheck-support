Safety boundaries

DailyCheck is not an emergency service, a monitoring service, a clinician, a pharmacy or a care provider. It does not observe anyone, and it cannot tell that a person is unwell, has fallen or needs help.

A day without a check-in is not evidence of anything. DailyCheck cannot detect absence and never reports one, so it must not be relied on to raise an alarm. It does not promise that a person you chose will see a check-in or respond to it.

If somebody is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For a medical symptom or a medicine question, contact an appropriate qualified professional.

Availability

DailyCheck tries to save each check-in locally before sharing it. A successful local save does not require a network connection. The app shows a failure if it cannot save the check-in. Sharing depends on iCloud and the chosen connection.

Delivery, invitations and notifications depend on Apple’s iCloud and on the recipient’s device and network. Apple may delay or group notifications. DailyCheck reports only what it has confirmed, and a check-in shown as waiting to send has not yet reached anyone.
