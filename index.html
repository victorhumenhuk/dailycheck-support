DailyCheck support

Effective September 18, 2026

DailyCheck is a private daily check-in for families who live apart. One person taps once a day to say they are okay. The people they chose see it on their own iPhone, through iCloud, and nobody else does.

Get help

Email dailycheck@thesmios.com and a person will read it. There is no account for us to look up, so say what you were doing and what you expected to happen, and mention which iPhone and iOS version you have if the problem looks technical.

Privacy questions, requests about your information, complaints and security reports go to the same address. Put “Privacy” or “Security” in the subject line and it reaches the right person faster.

Common questions

How do I invite someone?

Open Settings, choose Your people, add their name, and send the invitation that opens. They accept it on their own iPhone. Up to four people can receive your check-ins, and none of them pay anything.

What does a day without a check-in mean?

Only that DailyCheck has no check-in for that day. DailyCheck shows the check-ins a person chooses to send and nothing more. It does not raise an alarm about a quiet day, and a quiet day is not a sign that something is wrong.

Where is my information?

On your iPhone, in an encrypted store, and in the private iCloud space of each person you chose. DailyCheck has no server of its own and no account to create, so there is nowhere else for it to be.

How do I stop sharing with someone?

Open Settings, choose Your people, choose the person, and stop their access. Their copy is cleared the next time they open DailyCheck.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then Delete everything. Every person’s access is stopped first and confirmed with iCloud, and only then is anything removed from your iPhone.
