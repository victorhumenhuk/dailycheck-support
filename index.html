DailyCheck support

Effective September 18, 2026

DailyCheck is a private daily check-in for families who live apart. One person taps once a day to say they are okay. The people they chose see it privately through iCloud, and nobody else does.

Get help

Email dailycheck@thesmios.com. A person reads every message. There is no account to look up, so tell us what you were doing and what you expected to happen.

Privacy questions, requests about your information, complaints and security reports go to the same address. Put “Privacy” or “Security” in the subject and it reaches the right person.

Common questions

How do I invite someone? Open Settings, choose Your people, add their name, and send the private invitation that opens. They accept it on their own iPhone. Up to four people can receive your check-ins, and they never pay anything.

A day without a check-in. DailyCheck shows only the check-ins a person chooses to send. A quiet day means DailyCheck has no check-in for that day. It does not mean anything is wrong, and DailyCheck never sends an alert about it.

Where is my information? On your iPhone, in an encrypted store, and in the private iCloud space of each person you chose. DailyCheck has no server of its own and no account to create.

How do I stop sharing with someone? Open Settings, choose Your people, choose the person, and stop their access. Their copy is cleared the next time they open DailyCheck.

How do I delete everything? Settings, then Delete everything. Every person’s access is stopped first, confirmed with iCloud, before anything is removed from your iPhone.
