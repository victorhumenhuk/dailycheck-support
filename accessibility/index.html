DailyCheck accessibility statement

Effective September 18, 2026

DailyCheck is designed to work with familiar iPhone accessibility features. The app uses Dynamic Type, large controls, VoiceOver labels, system appearance, reduced-motion-aware presentation and an in-app accessibility settings route where those facilities are available.

Feedback and help

If something is difficult to use, email dailycheck@thesmios.com with the device, iOS version, screen and accessibility setting involved. We will use that information to investigate and prioritize improvements.

Current status

This statement does not claim a formal WCAG conformance level. Before a public release, test the signed app at default, XXL and accessibility text sizes; with VoiceOver, reduced motion, darker appearance and iPhone permission flows; and on current supported physical devices. Publish a refreshed statement with the test date, scope, known limitations and a response route.
